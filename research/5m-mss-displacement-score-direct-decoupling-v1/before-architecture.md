# Before architecture

The retired 5m path was:

`closed candle -> 2L/2R structural lifecycle -> MSS event -> additive Displacement score -> leg -> WATCH -> FVG FIRST_TOUCH`

Displacement eligibility added four independent price votes and one same-candle MSS vote, then compared the sum with `minScore=3`. A candle with only two passing price features could therefore exist as Displacement solely because any same-bar MSS added one point, including 184 opposite-direction cases in the frozen audit population.

Direct consumers also carried the obsolete abstraction into AMD, delivery Bias, FVG scoring, Entry scoring, WATCH snapshots, opportunity identity/quality, persistence diagnostics, and notification wording.

The frozen BTCUSDT/ZECUSDT USD-M Futures fixtures contain 18,578 closed 5m candles. The old runtime produced 4,559 Displacements, 850 unique WATCH objects, and 506 FIRST_TOUCH objects.
